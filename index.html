01 About

Hey, I'm Scroogio. I speedrun Minecraft and post the highs, the lows and the "how did that even happen" moments.

Most of what I do is MCSR Ranked: head-to-head races to kill the Ender Dragon as fast as possible on the same seed. I chase PBs, grind private rooms and break down the runs that went right (and very wrong).

You'll also find random-seed glide runs, bastion routing, Minecraft Bingo, achievement runs and the occasional tutorial with a map download.
